Are You Ready To Be Remembered?

A lot of you old-school reps out there, this is something we always had to do. If you go back to the late '90s, early 2000s, you always had your brag book. Your brag book is your list of all your accomplishments. So, usually it would be printing out your numbers, what you were doing well, your rankings, all the stuff we talked about. It could be positive emails from a manager. It could be promotions that you printed out in an email, letters of recommendation from physicians. Whatever it is, you compile it, put it all into a folder, and then you present your brag book in the interview. But, over the years that kind of went to the wayside, where a lot of managers don't expect it. But, still I'd rather see you go in there with this brag book, because no one does this anymore, guys. They don't. Managers don't ask for it, but just because they don't ask for it anymore, doesn't mean you shouldn't create that. Because you are going to stand out when you have that.
