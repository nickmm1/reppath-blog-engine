Are You Confusing Pharma And Device?

Pharma and device are not the same job. So, when you go to a recruiter and say, "Yeah, I'm I'm open to either one." Now, that's a red flag to a recruiter and definitely to a hiring manager cuz that means that you didn't do your homework. If you say they're the same job, you did not do your homework, guys. You could be open to either, but you really have to have an understanding of what that means because depending on which path you choose, it's going to be a completely different lifestyle. So, I want to get rid of that myth that a lot of people think, "I don't know. I maybe I'll do pharma, maybe I'll do device." That's like saying to someone, "Yeah, you know what? Maybe I'll play football or maybe I'll play tennis." They're both sports, right? These are both medical sales. It's both under the medical sales umbrella, but they're completely different careers.
