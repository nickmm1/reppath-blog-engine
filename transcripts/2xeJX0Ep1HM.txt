Still sending resumes and hoping blindly?

Start with an opening first, and make sure that you're qualified or at least realistically potentially qualified. If you don't meet at least 50% of those requirements, and I think it should be up to 60% or 70% because there's too many candidates out there, guys, that they can ignore you forever. You want to make sure that you're at least somewhat of a fit and meet at least half of those requirements. And then when you send your resume for that whatever opening it is, to start contacting immediately individuals that work at that company. Sales reps, managers, different personnel. I don't care who it is. Ideally, you want to call on the sales reps that work with the manager you're going to interview with, but it could be other individuals there, too. But start having conversations with people that are in that division, that are in the know, that know what it's like to work with that hiring manager. Have those conversations. That's networking.
