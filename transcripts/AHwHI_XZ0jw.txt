Why Are You Still Making Excuses?

For all you candidates out there that are trying to break into medical sales, remember the application process is the same as the sales process. So, many of you like to make excuses and say, "Yeah, but Joe, I don't have sales experience. I don't have a clinical background. You know, I always lose the job to someone that has more." Guys, remember, if you can't sell your way through the interview process, why should they give you a job? So, when they give you a job, you're not going to have all the advantages of your product. It's going to have more advantages over everyone else's products. So, you got to be a salesman and sell your product, outsell your competition. You're not going to have the perfect product, just like you're not going to be the perfect candidate in the application process. So, you have to learn how to treat it like a sales process and sell yourself to convince them that you're the best candidate. Once you realize that, guys, you'll move further through these processes and finally land a position.
