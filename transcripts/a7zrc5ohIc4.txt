Treat the job search like it's a sales funnel. You got to look at it the same way and talk in that lingo. You got to talk the talk, too, when you're speaking to reps, when you're speaking to recruiters and hiring managers. This is the way sales reps talk. They don't just say, "I'm applying to a ton of jobs." A sales rep has everything broken down, where they know how many calls they make each week. That's how they speak. So, the same thing, I applied to 15 jobs this week. I heard back from six of them. I got two interviews booked, and I'm in the second interview with one of the companies. Start thinking like that, like it's a sales funnel. This is a way that a salesman talks. Start talking like a salesman. Start treating it this way.
