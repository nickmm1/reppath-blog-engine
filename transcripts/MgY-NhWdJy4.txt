Why Are You Pitching Too Soon?

Don't mention your product on the first call. Fight with that. Fight yourself. It's one of the toughest things that sales reps have to do. They love to talk about their product. They love to talk about the name. They love to talk about the features and benefits. But when you get time with that doctor, especially someone you've been trying to get in front of for weeks or months at a time, and finally he says come in the back, I got a couple minutes. Now you're like, "Oh I got to spit out all the information about my product." But if you fight it, you know, again, if the doctor says, "What are you selling?" You could touch on it and be like, "We'll get right into that in a minute, but I'm here because I want to learn more about what's important to you and your practice and your patients." You redirect it that way, and then start asking a lot of questions, and make it your goal to learn as much as you can to get a better understanding about that doctor. Think of the doctor's mentality. The doctor doesn't come in and meet with a patient and go, "I'm going to tell you everything we do. I'm going to tell you all the services I provide." They don't jump in and start talking about everything they can do for that patient, because they're technically a sales rep, too. Don't forget, a lot of these doctors, there's a lot of elective surgeries that now a patient's in front of them, they can't just go in and say, "I'm going to do this procedure on you. You're going to love it." No, they ask tons of questions. They're seeing what your needs are, and then they're providing a solution to solve your needs. It's that simple, guys. Notice that the next time, hopefully you don't have to go to the doctor, but whenever you're going to the doctor for, you know, a minor cold or whatever it is, they say, "Tell me what's going on." I'm not going to say you're going to walk into a doctor's office and say, "Tell me what's going on." But essentially you're going to have questions that are structured in that way to learn more about that doctor in the practice. Again, once you do that, once you have that information, then you can provide the solution.
