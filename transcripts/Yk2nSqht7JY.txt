Are You Applying Without A Strategy?

One of the first things that we teach all of our candidates at the Repath Academy, they are not allowed to just apply to a job. Of course, I'm sure that they still do because they see something, but that isn't the right approach to just do the spray and pray technique and just send your resume out to hundreds of openings. It doesn't work, guys. A lot of you out there are going, "I've done that. How come I constantly get ghosted? No one even gets back to me." Even if they do, they get back to me with an automated response. "Thank you for applying. Unfortunately, you know, we decided to go with another candidate." Whatever the canned line is, but at the end of the day, you got nowhere. So, absolute non-negotiable, you apply to jobs that you are qualified for. I know this sounds crazy, guys. You have to be within a stone's throw away of being qualified. So, you have to read the requirements.
