Can You Prove What Went Wrong?

How do you mentally handle a quarter where you did everything right, but the numbers just didn't come through because of factors outside of your control? >> I mean, first you got to take a real assessment. Is that real or you lying to yourself? Are you really doing everything right? You know, is there really nothing more you can do? Are you really coming in with the right messaging? Typically, when you're not hitting your number, of course, there's always things that are outside of control. But if you really are honest with yourself, and even your managers are like, "Hey Joe, I don't get it. You're doing everything right. I'm with you. I agree with the messaging. I agree with the game plan." Sometimes doctors get slow. Sometimes they don't have that patient coming in that uses your device every week. And it could happen. But I think having that honest assessment with yourself and really, again, this is why you always got to control and own your territory. Really own it. And there's a big difference between just managing a territory and having full ownership of it. When you actually have that ownership and you know what's going on, then you'll be able to say, "Hey, you know what? I did fall short because Dr. Jones, who usually does five cases a month with us, he was away all month." And this is real. And you could have a couple of doctors that that happens. If you could show the numbers, like, "I lost 12 cases last quarter because of X, Y, and Z. That's where we fell short $300,000. But this quarter, we're going to more than double that because that's not going to happen and those cases are going to be booked, you know, I already have them booked ahead of time." No company is going to give you for that. But when you start reach, "I don't know. I mean, it was crazy. Last week it rained all week or, you know, it was whatever holiday." And when you start reaching for that nonsense, you're not being honest with yourself. Your manager's going to see through it. Just be honest with yourself, but have that ownership where if it's okay to fall short, you just got to know why you fell short.
