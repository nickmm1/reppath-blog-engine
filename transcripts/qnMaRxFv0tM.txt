Are You Invisible Inside Your Company?

You don't just get a medical sales role and then the networking stops or you're only networking with physicians. I made this mistake when I started. I was just so focused on building my network, building my base of business with all my doctors and I really didn't do a great job of networking within my company. I had friends, I knew some people in HR, I knew some people in marketing and all that, but I could have done a much better job looking back to really make sure that my networking internally is key and I'm always working on that because those relationships, guys, are going to help you get to that next level when it comes to promotions.
