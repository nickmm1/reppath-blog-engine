Why Should They Choose You?

The interview process is a sales call. Who am I going to hire? Someone that does have some experience, but didn't reach out to people, didn't go out in front of doctors to see learn more about this product, gave me a 30-60-90 that was generic, or the person that shows their hunger, shows their work ethic, has had multiple conversations, has given me inside info about what are the real challenges in this role, has gone out there and tried to have conversations with doctors to learn what their thoughts are on this product, had a 30-60-90 that they put together that's very specific for this territory. You do that, guys, you are light years ahead of everyone else.
